# Demo playbook (Devpost video / live demo)

## The notes to paste

Tested end to end: 10 ideas, ~10 s to fill, every scene carries its amendment's number image
(1 bun, 2 shoe, 3 tree, 4 door, 5 hive, 6 sticks, 7 heaven/angel, 8 gate, 9 wine, 10 bun + hero),
and **stop N holds amendment N**.

```
The Bill of Rights:
1st Amendment: freedom of speech, religion and the press.
2nd Amendment: the right to bear arms.
3rd Amendment: soldiers can't be housed in your home.
4th Amendment: no searches without a warrant.
5th Amendment: you can stay silent instead of testifying against yourself.
6th Amendment: a speedy trial with a lawyer.
7th Amendment: a jury trial in civil lawsuits.
8th Amendment: no cruel or unusual punishment.
9th Amendment: rights not listed still belong to the people.
10th Amendment: powers not given to the federal government belong to the states.
```

Why this one: everyone half-knows it but can't recite it; the numbers show off the number-rhyme
system memory athletes use; and a judge can be quizzed live ("What's the 4th Amendment?" →
stop 4 → a door slams on a hand searching without a warrant).

Keep each line short and in this shape: the "1st Amendment:" prefix is what lines the numbers up
with the stops, and a list of more than 4 items gets split into two stops (which shifts the rest).

## Before recording

1. Settings → "How weird should scenes get?" → **Just silly** (judge-friendly).
2. Build the demo palace on the final 3 Reitz panoramas. To show walking between rooms, **plant the
   10 eggs yourself first** (say 4 / 3 / 3 across the rooms, on memorable objects, in walking order):
   eggs you plant go first, otherwise all 10 ideas land in the first room.
3. Fill it once with the notes above, wander through once so every picture is drawn, and quiz once.
   Everything is saved, so the live run is instant and can't hit a Gemini outage. Filling the same
   notes on the same stops again is free and instant too.
4. Do this on the demo laptop (or copy `backend/data/` and `backend/loci.db` to it).

## Story (3 minutes)

1. 20 s: the problem: notes don't stick; memory athletes use palaces, but building one is the hard part.
2. 40 s: paste the notes (or show "Photo or PDF" with a printed page) → "Your palace is ready!"
3. 60 s: Wander 3–4 stops: point out the door at stop 4, the hive at stop 5 (4 = door, 5 = hive).
4. 40 s: Quiz: ask a judge "who lives at the chess set?"; show "Close! Did you have it?" and Round 2.
5. 20 s: night mode / dyslexia font, spaced review, campus route.
