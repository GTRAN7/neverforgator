# Demo playbook (Devpost video / live demo)

## Two examples

**Built in (already filled, opens from "See a finished example" and the tour): parts of a cell.**
10 organelles in the game room, each at an object that already does its job: the cell membrane at the
theater doors ("members only"), lysosomes in the recycling bins (Lysol-like juice), the nucleus on the
TV ("NEW CLUES!"), the Golgi on the putting green (a golfer putting gift boxes into holes). The atrium
and hallway stay empty to show a palace can grow. Great for quizzing a judge ("what does the Golgi do?").

**Generated live in the demo: Greek gods.** No numbers, everyone knows a few, and each god shows up as
themselves with their symbol, at an object that fits (Ares starts a war on the chess set, Poseidon putts a
ball into the ocean on the putting green). Easy to quiz a judge: "who's the god of war?"

```
Greek gods and what they rule:
Zeus is king of the gods and god of the sky and thunder.
Poseidon is god of the sea and earthquakes.
Hades rules the underworld.
Athena is goddess of wisdom and strategy.
Ares is god of war.
Aphrodite is goddess of love and beauty.
Hermes is the messenger of the gods.
Apollo is god of music and the sun.
```

Backup if you want something everyone already knows (more reliable scenes, less "wow"):

```
The 8 planets in order from the Sun:
Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
Venus is the hottest planet because of its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere.
Earth is the only planet known to have life.
Mars is red because of rust (iron oxide) on its surface.
Jupiter is the biggest planet, with a giant storm called the Great Red Spot.
Saturn has the brightest rings, made of ice and rock.
Uranus spins on its side.
Neptune is the windiest planet.
```

Shape your own notes like these: one short line per thing to remember. A numbered line stays one stop
(so the numbers line up with the stops); a list of more than 4 items gets split.

## Before recording

1. "How weird should scenes get?" is **Just silly** by default (judge-friendly).
2. Build the demo palace on the final 3 Reitz panoramas. To show walking between rooms, **plant the
   10 eggs yourself first** (say 4 / 3 / 3 across the rooms, on memorable objects, in walking order):
   eggs you plant go first, otherwise all 10 ideas land in the first room.
3. Rehearse the live fill once on the same stops: results are saved, so the real live run is instant
   and can't hit a Gemini "high demand" outage (and shows the same scenes you rehearsed with).
   Wander through once so every picture is drawn.
4. Do this on the demo laptop (or copy `backend/data/` and `backend/loci.db` to it).

## Story (3 minutes)

1. 20 s: the problem: notes don't stick; memory athletes use palaces, but building one is the hard part.
2. 40 s: paste the Greek gods (or show "Photo or PDF" with a printed page) → "Your palace is ready!"
3. 60 s: Wander 3–4 stops: point out Zeus at the foosball table, Ares on the chess set.
4. 40 s: Quiz: ask a judge "who's the god of war?"; show "Close! Did you have it?" and Round 2.
   (Or open the built-in cell and ask "what does the cell membrane do?")
5. 20 s: night mode / dyslexia font, spaced review, campus route.
